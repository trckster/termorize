A little Italian, every day.

Sometimes the best way to learn a language is to slow down and notice the words around you.

In the evening, take a passeggiata through the neighbourhood. There is no destination in mind: just a walk, a familiar face, a moment to catch up.

Small rituals have a vocabulary of their own. Keep a word that catches your attention, then try using it tomorrow.
