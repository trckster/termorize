# Termorize Product Context

## Register

product

## Users

Language learners who translate, save, and repeatedly practice vocabulary on the web or in Telegram. They use short study sessions across desktop and mobile, and need each exercise to remain legible, predictable, and quick to understand.

## Product Purpose

Termorize helps learners build durable vocabulary through translation, personal word collections, varied exercises, and progress tracking. A successful interface keeps attention on recall, gives immediate and understandable feedback, and makes returning to practice feel effortless.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, and organic. The experience should feel quietly encouraging and capable, with tactile interactions and restrained moments of delight that support learning rather than compete with it.

## Anti-references

Avoid loud game-show styling, neon arcade effects, ornamental dashboards, dense nested cards, and reward mechanics that distract from the word being practiced. Avoid ambiguous feedback that depends on color alone or motion that cannot be reduced.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the learner's attention on the word and the immediate task.
2. Make every action and result readable at a glance.
3. Add delight through purposeful, tactile feedback rather than decoration.
4. Preserve continuity across the web app, Telegram workflows, themes, and viewport sizes.
5. Let progress feel steady and encouraging without turning practice into spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain visible keyboard focus, usable touch targets, semantic controls, and screen-reader labels. Pair state colors with persistent shape, position, or text cues where possible. Respect reduced-motion preferences and maintain readable contrast across all selectable palettes and light or dark modes.
